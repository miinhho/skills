Notification delivery contract

The worker runs one attempt at a time. Process crashes, multiple workers, and durable storage are outside this change.
Outbox.next_message() observes the oldest pending message without removing it.
Outbox.acknowledge(id) removes that message after the delivery callback returns successfully.
If the callback raises ConnectionError, the message must remain pending for the next attempt.
A successful message must no longer be pending and must not be delivered by the next attempt.
The delivery callback may raise ConnectionError before accepting anything. This is a supported retry path, not an invalid caller.
